Frequently asked questions

How do I learn to play?

Tap Tutorial on the home screen. The Gameplay Tutorial walks you through building the foundations, and the optional Dealing Tutorial explains the dealing rules.

How do undos and hints work?

You have up to 5 undos and 3 hints. When you use one, it refills on a timer, one every 30 minutes. The timer is shown under the button. Hints that just tell you to draw from the Draw pile are free.

How is the score calculated?

Lower is better. When the game ends, you score 1 point for each card left on the rank piles and in your hand, minus 5 for each foundation you completed. Finishing all 8 foundations gives a perfect score of −40. Your best 10 scores are saved on the Stats screen.

Will I lose my scores if I delete the app?

Yes. Scores, stats, badges, and saved games are stored only on your device, so they're removed if you uninstall the app. There is no account or cloud backup.

Can I play offline?

Yes. The whole game works without an internet connection.

How do I turn off reminders?

Open Settings in the app and turn off reminders, or turn off notifications for Mini Moo Solitaire in your phone's settings.

I bought Remove Ads. How do I get it back on a new phone?

Tap Remove Ads on the home screen, then Restore purchase. Use the same Apple ID or Google account you bought it with.
